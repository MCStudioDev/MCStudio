500MW Grid-Scale Energy Storage Project

Location: Midwest, USA

Duration: 18 Months

Investment: $150 Million

Challenges

  • Integrating large-scale storage with existing grid infrastructure
  • Meeting stringent reliability and safety requirements
  • Optimizing for multiple revenue streams (frequency regulation, peak shaving, renewable firming)
  • Navigating complex regulatory environment

Solution

  • Designed a modular battery system architecture for scalability
  • Implemented advanced battery management system with AI-based optimization
  • Developed comprehensive grid integration strategy
  • Created detailed financial model for revenue optimization

Results

  • Grid Stability: Reduced frequency deviations by 60%
  • Renewable Integration: Enabled 200MW additional solar capacity
  • Revenue Generation: Achieved 15% IRR through optimized market participation
  • Reliability: 99.8% system availability
