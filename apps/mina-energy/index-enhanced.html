Technical Expertise

Battery State Estimation

SOC/SOH/SOP estimation using AI-based and physics-based hybrid models for accurate battery performance prediction. Our advanced algorithms provide unparalleled accuracy in battery state monitoring.

Digital Twin Modeling

Development of digital twins for NMC cells, modules, and packs using physics-based and machine learning approaches. We create virtual replicas that enable predictive analysis and optimization.

Thermal Management

Electro-thermal modeling and surface temperature estimation under driving and fast-charge conditions. Our thermal solutions ensure optimal battery performance and safety.

Fleet Analytics

Integration of SOC, SOH, and RUL models for predictive maintenance and customer insights in battery fleets. We help you maximize fleet efficiency and minimize downtime.

EV & BESS Integration

System design and integration for electric vehicle charging architectures and battery energy storage systems. Our solutions enable seamless integration of EV and storage technologies.

Renewable Energy Systems

Grid-connected energy storage optimization, DER allocation, and network reconfiguration for enhanced reliability. We optimize renewable energy systems for maximum efficiency and reliability.
