Content Optimization

Keyword Strategy

Research and implement relevant keywords for energy storage consulting services.

  • Primary keywords: "energy storage consulting", "battery systems"
  • Long-tail keywords: "BESS project development", "utility-scale storage"
  • Local keywords: "energy storage consultant [location]"

On-Page SEO

Optimize page titles, meta descriptions, headers, and content structure.

  • Title tags under 60 characters
  • Meta descriptions under 160 characters
  • Proper H1, H2, H3 hierarchy
  • Keyword placement in first 100 words

Image Optimization

Optimize images for web performance and accessibility.

  • Compress images without quality loss
  • Add descriptive alt text
  • Use proper file formats (WebP, JPEG, PNG)
  • Implement lazy loading

Social Media Integration

Optimize content for social sharing and engagement.

  • Open Graph meta tags
  • Twitter Card meta tags
  • Social sharing buttons
  • Schema markup for rich snippets
