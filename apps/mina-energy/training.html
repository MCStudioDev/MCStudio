Featured Courses

Battery Technology Fundamentals

4 Weeks | 20 Hours Beginner Level Online Certificate Included

Course Overview

This comprehensive course covers the fundamental principles of battery technology, from basic electrochemistry to advanced system design. Participants will gain a solid foundation in battery operation, safety, and applications.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand battery electrochemistry and cell design
  • Learn about different battery chemistries and their applications
  • Master battery management system fundamentals
  • Understand safety considerations and best practices
  • Gain practical knowledge through case studies and examples

Course Modules

Module 1 Introduction to Battery Technology 3 Hours
Module 2 Battery Chemistry and Design 4 Hours
Module 3 Battery Management Systems 5 Hours
Module 4 Safety and Standards 4 Hours
Module 5 Applications and Case Studies 4 Hours

Grid Integration & Operations

6 Weeks | 30 Hours Advanced Level Hybrid Format Professional Certificate

Course Overview

This advanced course focuses on the technical and operational aspects of integrating energy storage systems with electrical grids. Participants will learn about grid codes, market participation, and operational strategies.

Learning Objectives

  • Master grid integration requirements and standards
  • Understand energy markets and revenue streams
  • Learn operational strategies for optimal performance
  • Develop skills in system monitoring and control
  • Gain insights into future grid evolution
