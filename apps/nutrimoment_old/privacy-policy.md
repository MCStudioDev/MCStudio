# Privacy Policy

NutriMoment is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

## 1. Data Collection
We collect:
- Photos you upload for analysis (processed temporarily and not stored permanently).
- Pantry items and health conditions you save (stored locally on your device/server).
- Language and cuisine preferences.

## 2. Data Usage
Your data is used solely to:
- Identify ingredients in your photos.
- Generate personalized recipe suggestions.
- Maintain your pantry and meal plan.

## 3. Third-Party Services
We use Google Gemini AI to process images and generate text. Your photos are sent to Google for processing.

## 4. Data Security
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your local data.

## 5. Contact
For questions, contact us at privacy@nutrimoment.app
